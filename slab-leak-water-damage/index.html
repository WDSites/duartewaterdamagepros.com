Emergency Slab Leak Water Damage Cleanup in Duarte, CA

A leak in a pipe under the concrete floor still soaks what sits on top of it. We remove the water and dry the rooms once a plumber has stopped the leak.

Slab leak water damage cleanup in Duarte, CA is the removal of water and the drying of floors, cabinets and walls after a pressurized pipe under or inside a concrete slab has leaked into the house. Dispatch is answered 24/7, which helps because these leaks rarely announce themselves at a convenient hour. Finding and fixing the pipe is a plumber's job. Ours starts when water reaches the living space.

What a Slab Leak Is, and What We Cannot Tell You

A slab leak is a leak in a water line that runs beneath or through the concrete foundation of a house. We cannot tell you what share of Duarte homes sit on a slab and what share have a raised foundation, and we will not guess.

If your home is on a slab, this page applies to you. If it has a raised floor, a leak is more likely to show up in the crawl space, which is a different job.

Slab leaks are located by a leak detection plumber, who uses listening equipment and pressure tests to pinpoint the pipe. We do not diagnose the pipe and we do not repair it. What we do is deal with the water that has already come up through cracks, expansion joints and wall bases, and with the materials that soaked it up.

A slab leak does not always announce itself. Water can travel under flooring for days, spread along the underlayment and wick up into drywall before a stain appears. That is why a moisture meter and thermal camera matter here. They show the wet boundary that your eyes cannot.

First-hour safety steps

Close the main shutoff valve at your house. If water is still arriving, the leak is likely between the meter and the house, so the water utility is the next call. Do not walk through water near outlets, cords or appliances, and turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

Call 911 for any electrical hazard or the smell of gas. Call a plumber who does leak detection to find the pipe. Then call this 24/7 line to start extraction and drying while the plumber works.

Who Owns Which Pipe

California American Water is the retail water provider for Duarte, and its responsibility ends at the meter. The line from the meter to your house, and every pipe in and under it, belongs to you. The utility publishes a number for water leaks in the street, 888-237-1333. Use it if water is surfacing outside, and call a plumber if the problem is on your side of the meter.

How We Dry a House After a Slab Leak

  1. Moisture mapping. A moisture meter and thermal camera trace how far the water spread through flooring, baseboards and the lower part of walls.
  2. Extraction. Extraction units and wet vacuums pull standing water off hard floors and out of carpet and pad.
  3. Removal of what cannot dry. Wet pad, swollen baseboard and soaked drywall at the base of walls may be cut out and bagged.
  4. Drying setup. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers go in, and floor drying mats or cavity drying systems are used where water sits under flooring.
  5. Monitoring. We return to read the meters until the materials reach a dry standard, following the published ANSI/IICRC S500 reference for water damage restoration.

Slab water can be hard to dry because concrete holds moisture for a long time. Tile and engineered wood over a wet slab need extra care, and some flooring will have to come up so the subfloor and slab surface can release water.

What Changes the Cost

  • Spread of the water. More rooms and more linear feet of wet wall mean more equipment and labor.
  • Flooring type. Carpet, laminate, engineered wood and tile dry or fail in different ways.
  • Cabinets and built-ins. Wet toe kicks and cabinet bases can sometimes be dried in place and sometimes cannot.
  • How long it ran. A leak that went unnoticed for days has soaked deeper than one caught at once.
  • Drying days. Concrete and thick flooring layers extend the monitoring period.

Insurance Fit

Coverage for slab leaks depends on the cause and how long the leak ran. A sudden and accidental release is often treated differently from a slow leak, and gradual damage is often excluded. Policies also differ on whether they pay for tearing into a slab to reach the pipe. Report the loss promptly, keep receipts, take photos and ask your insurer what applies. We do not promise approval or payout.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Covering a damp floor with new flooring before it has been dried and measured.
  • Running only a household fan, which moves air but does not pull moisture out of concrete or cavities.
  • Waiting for a stain before calling, when a warm spot or a spinning meter already tells you something is wrong.
  • Treating the cleanup as finished when the plumber leaves.
