Where the Mel Canyon Area Is

The area sits at the northern edge of Duarte, below Fish and Opal canyons. Mel Canyon Road is the anchor street, spelled Melcanyon in some county documents, and Deerlane Drive runs nearby. Glenn Miller Park, 205 Melcanyon Drive, is a handy landmark when you describe your location to dispatch.

Local context: the Fish Fire and mudflow alerts

The Fish Fire burned about 39 acres in undeveloped Fish and Opal canyons on June 12, 2022 and was contained that day. A Los Angeles County Public Works brief named Melcanyon Road and Deerlane Drive as streets where debris flows could leave sediment, with city K-rails limiting access impacts to 44 residential properties. Check county burn-area guidance before each forecast storm.

CBS Los Angeles reported a Phase 2 mudflow alert for that burn scar on January 4 to 6, 2023, with parking restrictions on both streets. ABC7 reported that on February 17, 2017, mud passed a row of K-rails on Mel Canyon Road and 202 homes in the impact area were ordered to evacuate, with no significant home damage reported. If you get an order, leave.

Storm and mud safety first

Follow LA County burn-area and evacuation guidance, and do not walk or drive into mud or debris, even if it looks shallow. We are not a debris-removal or grading contractor, so mud outside is a job for the city, the county or a grading contractor.

Inside the home, shut off the water only if the valve is safe and reachable. Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords and appliances, and never operate breakers while standing in water. Mud and sewage need professionals. Photograph the damage from a safe spot, and call 911 for any life-threatening situation.

How Extraction and Drying Work for Storm Water

When muddy runoff gets through a door or garage, the first job is getting the water out. Extraction units pull standing water from floors, and a HEPA vacuum handles fine silt. The published ANSI/IICRC S500 industry reference treats runoff carrying soil as contaminated, which affects what is cleaned and what is removed.

Next a moisture meter and a thermal camera map where water traveled under baseboards, into wall bottoms and beneath flooring. Porous items such as carpet pad and wet insulation are often removed, while hard surfaces and framing are cleaned and treated.

Then air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the structure, with readings rechecked each visit. The method is on the structural drying page, and flood damage restoration covers larger events.

Insurance and Documentation

Coverage depends on the cause and your policy, so ask your insurer. California Department of Insurance guidance says homeowners policies typically exclude flood, mudslide and debris flow, so mudflow and flood are usually separate. A burst pipe may be treated differently.

Take dated photos from a safe spot, save moisture readings and the itemized scope, and keep receipts for emergency steps. A National Flood Insurance Program policy typically takes about 30 days to become active. We do not decide coverage.
