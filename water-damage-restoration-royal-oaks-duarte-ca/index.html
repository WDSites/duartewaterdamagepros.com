Homes, apartments and renters

The Census puts about 39 percent of Duarte households as renters, a citywide figure. If you rent near Royal Oaks, shutting off water and calling us is only part of the job. Your landlord decides on repairs to the building, while your renters policy may cover your belongings.

First-hour safety steps

If it is safe and you can reach it, shut off the water at the fixture or the main valve. Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords or appliances, and never operate a breaker while you are standing in water.

Water from a sewer line or toilet overflow is contaminated, and cleanup needs professionals. Photograph the damage from a dry spot. If water reaches the panel or you smell gas, leave and call 911 first.

How extraction and drying work in a Royal Oaks home

The sequence is the same for a house or an apartment. Standing water comes out first with extraction units. Then a moisture meter and a thermal camera map where water went, including under flooring and inside walls.

Air movers push dry air across wet surfaces, and LGR dehumidifiers pull moisture out of the air so materials can release what they hold. Readings are checked on each visit until they match dry material.
