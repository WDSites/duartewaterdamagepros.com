About Duarte Water Damage Pros

A 24/7 dispatch line for water damage and mold work in Duarte and nearby San Gabriel Valley towns.

☎ (626) 879-0276

Duarte Water Damage Pros takes calls about water damage and mold in Duarte, California, and dispatches licensed and insured restoration crews. We answer at any hour because water damage does not wait for business hours, and moisture left in walls and flooring invites mold within a day or two.

How a Call Works

You call (626) 879-0276 and describe what you see. Dispatch asks where the water started, how far it has spread and whether power or sewage could be involved. You get safety steps for the minutes before the crew arrives, and a crew is sent as soon as one is available. We do not promise an arrival time, because traffic, storms and call volume change it.

At the property, the crew assesses with moisture meters and a thermal camera and writes a scope. The scope and price are explained before work starts, and photos and readings are kept for your insurer.

What We Are and What We Are Not

We are a local-focused dispatch line backed by licensed and insured crews. We are not an insurance company and we cannot promise a claim will be approved. We are not plumbers, roofers or waterproofing contractors, and we say so when the right fix belongs to another trade. We do not provide independent mold testing.

Standards We Reference

Two published standards guide this work. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the standard for professional water damage restoration, and ANSI/IICRC S520 is the standard for professional mold remediation. Ask any company, including us, which standard its crews follow.

What a Visit Looks Like

The technician starts by walking the affected area with you and asking how the water arrived. Moisture meters are pressed against drywall, baseboards and floors, and a thermal camera shows cool, wet areas that do not look wet. The crew then sets up extraction, air movers and dehumidifiers according to the written scope.

On later visits the technician rechecks the same spots and writes down the readings. The equipment stays until those readings reach the dry standard, and not before. If something needs rebuilding, that is quoted as its own stage so you can compare it against your policy and decide who should do it.

Why Fast Drying Is the Whole Point

Wet building materials do not stay harmless while you decide what to do. Drywall wicks water upward, insulation holds it, and wood framing absorbs it slowly and releases it slowly. The EPA and the CDC both advise drying water-damaged areas and items within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, which is why a late-night phone call is not an overreaction.

It also explains why we ask so many questions on the first call. The answers decide which equipment goes on the truck. A clean supply-line leak that was caught in minutes needs different gear than a sewage backup that ran overnight, and a ceiling stain after a storm needs different gear than a flooded garage.

What We Document

For every job, the crew records the information an adjuster, a landlord or a lender is likely to ask for. That includes dated photos of the damage before anything is moved, moisture readings that show where the water traveled, the water category, the equipment placed and the days it ran, and a written scope that separates drying from repair. Keep your own copies of receipts for any emergency steps you take before the crew arrives.

How to Compare Restoration Companies

These questions work for any company, including this one.

  • Does the contract show a California contractor license number? The Contractors State License Board requires a license for work totaling $1,000 or more in labor and materials.
  • Which published standard do the crews follow? ANSI/IICRC S500 covers water damage and S520 covers mold.
  • Is the scope written and itemized, with drying separated from repair?
  • What documentation will you receive for your insurer?
  • What does the company not do, and who does it refer you to?

A company that cannot answer these plainly is a company to question further. See our services overview for what each stage includes.

Honest Limits

We would rather lose a job than promise something we cannot deliver. We cannot guarantee that an insurer will pay a claim, we cannot promise an arrival time, and we cannot tell you over the phone what a repair will cost. What we can promise is a written scope before work starts, readings that show when drying is finished and a plain answer when the right fix belongs to someone else.
