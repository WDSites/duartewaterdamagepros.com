What Arcadia's Own Safety Plan Says About Water

Arcadia's December 2024 Safety Element is unusually direct about flooding. It states that no part of Arcadia lies within a 100-year floodplain and that no residential area sits in a current FEMA flood zone. That is useful news, and it also changes what a typical Arcadia water loss looks like. Most calls come from plumbing, roofs, appliances and storm drains rather than from a river overflowing.

The same document describes the Santa Anita Dam, built between 1925 and 1927, as the facility with the greatest flooding impact potential, with a failure path that would cross roughly the eastern half of the city. It places Arcadia within the inundation areas of six dams and reservoirs. You cannot change that, but you can check your own address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask your insurer whether flood coverage makes sense.

When Water Is Spreading in Arcadia

If water is already on your floor, nothing above matters until it is stopped. These steps buy time while the crew is on the way.

Stop the source

Close the valve at the fixture or at the main. The city owns Arcadia's water system, so a break on the street side of the meter is a Public Works call.

Mind the electrical panel

Do not stand in water to reach it. If water is near outlets or the panel, call 911 or the utility first.

Clear the floor

Lift valuables and furniture, pull up loose rugs and keep pets and children out of dirty water.

Dial the 24/7 line

Say where the water started and whether it could be sewage. Dispatch will tell you what to do until the crew arrives.

Arcadia Basics for Homeowners

The City of Arcadia owns and operates both and bills them monthly, with a rate adjustment that took effect January 1, 2026. Monthly bills make leak spikes easier to spot.

Permits

The Building Services Division at 240 West Huntington Drive handles permits online through the EDGE portal.

Emergency response

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, including holidays.

Housing

About 58 percent of households own and about 42 percent rent, with a median gross rent of $2,191.

A monthly bill is a small tool with a practical payoff. A sudden rise on the water line, with no change in how the household lives, is often the first sign of a hidden leak, so compare the current bill with the one before.

Debris, Burn Scars and the North End

Northern Arcadia sits at the foot of Santa Anita Canyon. After the 2020 Bobcat Fire, the city's Safety Element notes that December 2021 storms caused a debris flow in northern Arcadia that washed about 300,000 cubic yards of sediment into Santa Anita Reservoir. Sediment removal at the Santa Anita Dam was completed in November 2024, according to the same document.

For homeowners, the lesson is practical. If you live near the canyon mouth, keep sandbags and a plan, and check your policy for mudflow wording before a storm. Our flood damage restoration team handles mud and contaminated water, and emergency water removal covers standing water of any source.
