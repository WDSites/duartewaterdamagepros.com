When Water Is Spreading in Monrovia

The first call usually comes from one of three situations. A supply line or water heater fails inside the house. A storm sends water or mud toward a foothill property. Or sewage backs up through a tub or floor drain. Each calls for a different first move, and dispatch will ask which one you are in.

Stop the water

Close the shutoff at the fixture or the main valve at the house. If the water is coming from the street side of the meter, that is a City of Monrovia utility issue.

Power and safety

Stay clear of wet outlets and the panel. If you cannot reach the panel on dry footing, call 911 or the utility first.

Lift and protect

Raise valuables, rugs and electronics off the floor and keep children and pets out of dirty water.

Call the 24/7 line

Tell dispatch where the water started, how far it has spread and whether it could include sewage.

Canyon Runoff and the Foothill Streets

Monrovia sits against the San Gabriel foothills, and storms that follow a wildfire can send debris downhill. In December 2021 a rainstorm sent mud and debris through Monrovia Canyon Park, the city declared a local emergency and the debris broke a water main. In January 2021, a storm over the Bobcat and Ranch 2 burn scars reached some roads, Canyon Park and the Hillside Wilderness Preserve, though news reports said the city avoided major residential damage.

Foothill homeowners should know that standard homeowners policies typically exclude flood and mudflow, so check your policy before a storm. Our flood damage restoration page covers mud, contaminated water and basements, and our emergency water removal page covers standing water of any source.

Utilities, Catch Basins and Who to Call

The city streets crew cleans storm water catch basins and open channels, so a clogged catch basin during a storm is a city report, not a restoration job. A failed main or a leak in the street also belongs to city Public Works. Water that has entered the house, whatever the source, is ours to remove.

One more local rule is worth checking. The city lists 164 properties as locally designated historic as of January 2024. Whether historic review applies to a specific address is a question for the Community Development Department before you replace original windows, trim or siding during repairs.
