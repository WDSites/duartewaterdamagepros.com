Contact Duarte Water Damage Pros

The fastest way to reach us is the phone. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day.

☎ (626) 879-0276

For water damage or mold, call (626) 879-0276. Dispatch is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. We do not use web forms, because a phone call gets a crew moving faster than a message waiting in an inbox.

What to Tell Dispatch

  1. Your address and the nearest cross streets.
  2. Where the water started and whether it is still running.
  3. How far it has spread, and which floors or rooms are wet.
  4. Whether it could be sewage or storm runoff.
  5. Whether any water is near outlets, appliances or the electrical panel.

If there is any electrical or gas risk, call 911 or the utility before calling us.
